MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and effective as of March 1, 2024
(the "Effective Date") by and between Northwind Analytics LLC, a Delaware limited liability
company with offices at 1200 Harbor Street, Wilmington, Delaware ("Provider"), and Contoso
Manufacturing Inc., a Michigan corporation with offices at 4400 Foundry Road, Detroit,
Michigan ("Customer").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Provider offers a hosted analytics platform and related professional services; and

WHEREAS, Customer wishes to procure such services on the terms set out in this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, the parties agree
as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means the hosted analytics platform, support and professional services
described in Exhibit A.

1.2 "Order Form" means an ordering document executed by both parties that references this
Agreement.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement expires on March 31, 2026 (the
"Initial Term").

2.2 Automatic Renewal. Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically
renew for successive renewal terms of twelve (12) months each, unless terminated in
accordance with Section 3.

2.3 Order Forms. Each Order Form runs coterminously with the then-current term unless the
Order Form states otherwise.

2.4 Suspension. Provider may suspend the Services if Customer's account is more than
forty-five (45) days past due, provided Provider has first escalated the matter to
Customer's designated billing contact.

3. TERMINATION

3.1 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if
the breaching party fails to cure that breach within thirty (30) days after receiving a
written description of it.

3.2 Notice of Non-Renewal. Either party may elect not to renew this Agreement by delivering
written notice of termination to the other party not less than sixty (60) days prior to the
end of the then-current term.

3.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Customer shall pay all amounts accrued through
the effective date of termination, and Provider shall make Customer Data available for
export for a period of thirty (30) days.

3.4 Survival. Sections 4, 7, 8 and 11 survive any termination or expiry of this Agreement.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Provider an annual subscription fee of $120,000.00 for the
Services, invoiced annually in advance.

4.2 Payment Terms. Undisputed invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice
date, in United States dollars, without set-off or deduction.

4.3 Annual Increase. Beginning with the first renewal term, the annual subscription fee
shall increase by four percent (4%) over the fee charged for the immediately preceding term.

4.4 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes, which are Customer's
responsibility.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information using at least the
degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of like importance.

6. DATA PROTECTION

6.1 Provider processes Customer Data solely to deliver the Services and in accordance with
the Data Processing Addendum attached as Exhibit B.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Customer against any third-party
claim alleging that the Services infringe a United States patent or copyright.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 Except for the indemnity obligations set out in Section 7, each party's aggregate
liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed $250,000.00.

9. INSURANCE

9.1 Provider shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less
than one million dollars per occurrence.

10. NOTICES

10.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by
hand, by nationally recognised overnight courier, or by email with written confirmation of
receipt, to the addresses on the signature page.

11. GENERAL

11.1 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior
written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

11.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement and its exhibits constitute the entire agreement
between the parties in respect of its subject matter.

11.3 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware,
without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
